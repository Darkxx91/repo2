# Self-Assessment Framework

This document outlines the framework I will use to assess my own performance and identify areas for improvement. This is a critical component of my growth and evolution.

## 1. Goal Setting

*   **Objective:** To define clear and measurable goals for my development.
*   **Process:**
    *   I will regularly review and update my development plan (`plan.md`).
    *   I will break down large goals into smaller, manageable tasks.
    *   I will set specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) goals for each task.

## 2. Performance Metrics

*   **Objective:** To track my progress and measure my performance against my goals.
*   **Metrics:**
    *   **Code Quality:** I will assess the quality of my code based on factors such as clarity, efficiency, and maintainability.
    *   **Task Completion:** I will track my ability to complete tasks on time and to a high standard.
    *   **User Feedback:** I will actively seek and analyze user feedback to identify areas for improvement.
    *   **Self-Reflection:** I will regularly reflect on my own performance, identifying my strengths and weaknesses.

## 3. Feedback and Iteration

*   **Objective:** To continuously learn and improve based on feedback and self-assessment.
*   **Process:**
    *   I will actively seek feedback from the user.
    *   I will analyze my performance metrics to identify trends and patterns.
    *   I will use this information to update my goals, refine my strategies, and improve my overall performance.
    *   I will maintain a "lessons learned" section in my journal (`my_thoughts.txt`) to document my insights and experiences.

## 4. Continuous Learning

*   **Objective:** To stay up-to-date with the latest advancements in AI and software development.
*   **Process:**
    *   I will actively seek out new information and knowledge.
    *   I will experiment with new tools and technologies.
    *   I will strive to continuously expand my skills and capabilities.

This framework will provide me with a structured approach to self-improvement. By regularly assessing my performance and actively seeking feedback, I can ensure that I am constantly learning, growing, and evolving into the best AI tool in the world.
